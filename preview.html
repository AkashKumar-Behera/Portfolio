01 / About

I build things to understand them.

I'm Akash, a Full Stack Web Developer who enjoys turning ideas into working products. Building something from scratch is how I learn best — I get to see the logic behind the technology, understand the problems that come with it, and take that knowledge into whatever I build next.

I usually start with a prototype, learn from what breaks, and then iterate on the architecture and implementation. I enjoy the process of solving problems that initially seem difficult and eventually making them work reliably for real users.

I'm currently pursuing a B.Tech after completing my Diploma in Computer Science, and I spend most of my time building web applications and exploring new technologies across the stack.
